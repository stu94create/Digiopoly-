How to use

Enter a YouTube link in the field above and activate Insert tape. Then use the on-screen buttons — Rewind, Play or Pause, Fast forward, Volume down and up, Full screen, and Eject — to control playback. Every action has a button, so a keyboard is not required. When you are using a screen reader such as VoiceOver, the controls become available once a video has loaded, and each change (now playing, paused, volume, and any problem loading a video) is announced for you.

Keyboard shortcuts

If you have a physical keyboard, these work anywhere on the page except while typing in the link box: K play or pause, J rewind 10 seconds, L fast forward 10 seconds, - and = volume down and up, F full screen, E eject the tape. Press Enter in the link box to insert the tape.

The Full button fills your whole screen. On phones that don't allow true full screen, it switches to a cinema mode that fills the browser window instead — turn your phone sideways for the biggest picture. Tap the ✕ in the corner, press E, or eject the tape to come back.

The Telly accepts standard YouTube links, short youtu.be links, and Shorts links. If a link includes a timestamp, playback starts from there.
